About

What PyVerse is, and why it works this way

Why this exists

Most Python material asks you to watch or read. PyVerse asks you to run: every exercise is graded in your browser, and every trace shows what actually happened in memory.

What is inside

  • Learn — short ordered courses with runnable examples.
  • Practice — graded challenges, curated sheets and topic tracks.
  • Labs — execute and inspect code step by step.
  • Blog — longer notes on the parts people get stuck on.

How it is built

The whole site is authored in Wagtail and exported as static files; Python runs in your browser through Pyodide. There is no account to create and nothing to install.
